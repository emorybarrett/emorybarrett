### Research Interests
Restoration aquaculture
  > Hatchery-scale production of ribbed mussels for use in nutrient bioextraction, bacterial remediation, and shoreline stabilization.

Ribbed mussel (_Geukensia demissa_) ecology
  > How can we use the unique ecology of ribbed mussels to inform aquaculture practices?

Mussel-microbe interactions
  > Ecological and evolutionary mechanics behind the diverse breadth of interactions between mussel species and bacteria.
